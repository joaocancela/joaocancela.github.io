About

Comparative questions, pragmatic methods

My main interests lie in comparative political behaviour. I have also published on political institutions, civil society, and elite attitudes. I take a problem-solving approach to political research, choosing methods that best fit each question—from spatial regression, survey experiments, and network analysis to archival research and focus groups.

In Spring 2024, I was the FLAD Visiting Professor in the Department of Government at Georgetown University. Previously, I taught at the University of Minho, worked as a policy officer at Portugal’s High Commission for Migration, and was a visiting PhD student at CIRCaP in Siena.
